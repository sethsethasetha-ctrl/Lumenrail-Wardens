Lumenrail Wardens Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Lumenrail Wardens is an offline game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what the app stores and what it does not collect.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account or use location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health, motion, or advertising identifier data.

Information stored on your device

The app stores game progress, unlocked levels, stars, upgrade materials, gameplay settings, music and sound preferences, vibration preference, accessibility preferences, and tutorial progress locally using the system preferences store. This data restores your local game experience and is not sent to us.

Audio

Music and sound effects play on your device. The app does not record audio and does not access the microphone.

Third-party services

Lumenrail Wardens does not include advertising, analytics, attribution, account, security, or other third-party data-collection SDKs. It does not use App Tracking Transparency and does not access IDFA.

Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you remove the app or clear its data through iOS. Because we do not receive or store personal information on a server, there is no remote account or server-side data to delete.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hengbozail@163.com.
